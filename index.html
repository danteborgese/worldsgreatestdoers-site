02 / About

What is a doer?

This channel exists to learn from the world's greatest doers.

Nassim Nicholas Taleb describes doers as people who operate through action, trial and error, and practical experience rather than abstract theory.

In his framework they have skin in the game: they carry the personal, financial, or physical risk of their own decisions.

Each episode profiles a legendary doer and deconstructs their actions, strategies, and techniques into frameworks you can apply in your work.

In theory there is no difference between theory and practice; in practice there is.
